About Me

I am a PhD candidate at Purdue University, where I study evolutionary biology in non-model organisms. My dissertation work uses whole-genome sequencing data to address critical challenges in wildlife conservation and to investigate evolutionary history through comparative genomics.

In my free time, find me outdoors with my dog, Maisie! I enjoy camping, hiking, birding, and rock climbing.
